Type Color — Privacy Policy

Effective 2026-05-27

What the extension does

  • Reads text on the web pages you visit, in your browser, only to recolor individual letters and digits. The reading happens locally on your device; no text is transmitted anywhere.
  • Saves your palette and per-site preferences using Chrome's built-in chrome.storage.sync API. This data stays in your Google account's Chrome profile. If you are signed in to Chrome on multiple devices, Chrome itself may sync this data between them. It is not sent to the extension developer or any third party.
  • Reads the URL of the active tab when you open the extension popup, only to show the hostname next to the per-site toggle.

What the extension does not do

  • It does not collect, transmit, or sell any personal data.
  • It does not contact any server controlled by the developer or by any third party.
  • It does not include analytics, telemetry, advertising, or tracking of any kind.
  • It does not read or modify form fields, password fields, or contenteditable content. These are skipped by design.

Permissions

  • Read and change all your data on websites you visit (host access, <all_urls>): required to recolor text on the sites where you use the extension. The extension only reads page text and inserts span wrappers to apply colors; it does not interact with the page in any other way.
  • storage: to save your palette and per-site toggles on your device.
  • activeTab: to read the URL of the currently active tab while the popup is open, used only to display the hostname in the per-site toggle.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at the same URL with a new effective date.
