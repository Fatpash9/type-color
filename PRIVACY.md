# Type Color — Privacy Policy

The current privacy policy lives at
**https://www.privacys.app/privacy/type-color**.

In short: Type Color reads page text only inside your browser to recolor
it, saves your palette in Chrome's built-in sync storage, and sends
nothing to any server. It has no analytics, ads, or tracking. The popup's
"Support" link opens Buy Me a Coffee only if you click it.

Questions: privacy@privacys.app
